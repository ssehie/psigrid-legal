Privacy Policy for PsiGrid

PsiGrid is developed and published by Steve Sehie. This Privacy Policy explains what information PsiGrid uses during closed testing and how that information is handled.

Short version: PsiGrid is a closed-test practice app. The app stores tester profile and message-preview activity locally on the device, may request location only if a tester chooses to fill in an approximate area, and does not sell personal information.

Information PsiGrid Handles

PsiGrid may handle the following information:

  • Profile setup details that a tester enters in the app, such as a display name, profile notes, and an optional approximate area.
  • Local preview activity, including draft practice state, direct message preview threads, and other tester-flow state used to make the closed-test build usable.
  • Approximate location information only when a tester chooses the in-app option to use the current area. Testers can also type an approximate area manually or skip the location feature.
  • Basic app diagnostics generated by the device or app platform, such as crash or install information supplied by Google Play.

How Information Is Used

PsiGrid uses information to:

  • Run the closed-test app experience on the tester's device.
  • Remember local profile, practice, and message-preview state.
  • Let testers optionally fill in a coarse location label such as a city or region.
  • Review app quality, reliability, usability, and tester feedback.

Location

PsiGrid requests location permission only when a tester taps the option to use the current area during profile setup. The app is designed to use this only for an approximate area label, not for tracking precise movement. Testers may deny location permission and continue using the app.

Storage and Sharing

Closed-test profile and message-preview state is stored locally on the tester's device. PsiGrid does not sell personal information. The app may access public web content, such as news or reference links, to display tester-facing resources.

Third-Party Services

PsiGrid is distributed through Google Play and may use platform services from Google Play for app delivery, crash reporting, and testing operations. Public links and feeds shown in the app are provided by their respective websites.

Children

PsiGrid is not directed to children. Closed-test access is intended for invited testers.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PsiGrid changes. The latest version will be posted at this page.
