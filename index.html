Privacy Policy for PsiGrid

PsiGrid is developed and published by Steve Sehie. This Privacy Policy explains what information PsiGrid handles in local preview mode and when a tester chooses to use live account and community features.

Short version: PsiGrid keeps preview activity on the device. If you sign in for live sync, PsiGrid uses Supabase to store account, profile, coarse opt-in location, message, room, safety, and deletion request data. PsiGrid does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Information PsiGrid Handles

PsiGrid may handle the following information:

  • Your email address and an account identifier when you request and complete an email sign-in link.
  • Profile information you choose to provide, including display name, biography, status, experience, practice interests, connection preferences, and an optional approximate area.
  • Device-local preview activity, such as practice progress, joined rooms, resource activity, draft conversations, and sample-data interactions.
  • Live community activity when signed in, including direct messages, room posts, profile blocks, safety reports, and account-deletion requests.
  • Coarse location information only when you choose to fill an area and separately enable location sharing. You may type an area, deny permission, or leave sharing off.
  • Basic delivery and diagnostic information supplied by app stores, operating systems, or testing platforms.

How Information Is Used

PsiGrid uses information to:

  • Provide local preview, training, profile, and practice features.
  • Authenticate users and synchronize opted-in profiles.
  • Provide profile discovery, direct messages, and room conversations.
  • Enforce block, report, moderation, and account-deletion workflows.
  • Review app quality, reliability, usability, and tester feedback.

Location

PsiGrid requests location permission only when a tester taps the option to use the current area during profile setup. The app is designed to round coordinates and publish them only when location sharing is enabled. PsiGrid is not designed to track movement or publish precise GPS history. You may deny location permission and continue using the app.

Storage, Sharing, and Security

Preview state is stored on your device. On supported native devices, live authentication sessions are stored using operating-system secure storage. Live account and community data is transmitted to and stored by the configured Supabase project. Public news and reference content may be requested from third-party websites. PsiGrid does not sell personal information.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase provides optional email authentication, database, and realtime synchronization.
  • Google Play and Apple services may provide app delivery, testing, and platform diagnostics.
  • Google News and linked public websites may provide news and reference content.

Retention and Deletion

Local preview data remains until you clear it, reset the app, clear app storage, or uninstall. Live data remains while needed to operate and protect the service. A signed-in user can submit an account deletion request in the app. Requests enter a manual review queue and are not completed immediately. To ask about a pending request, use the contact address below. Safety, fraud-prevention, legal, or audit records may be retained when reasonably necessary.

Children

PsiGrid is not directed to children under 13. Tester access is intended for invited users who can follow the community rules.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as PsiGrid changes. The latest version will be posted at this page.
